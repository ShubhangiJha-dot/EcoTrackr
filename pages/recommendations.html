Carbon Footprint Reduction Solutions

Here are some general and practical solutions to reduce your carbon footprint.

Transportation

  • Use public transport like metro or bus instead of private vehicles.
  • Prefer walking or cycling for short distances.
  • Carpool to reduce fuel consumption.

Home Energy Usage

  • Reduce AC usage and set temperature between 24-26°C.
  • Switch off lights and appliances when not in use.
  • Use energy-efficient appliances and LED bulbs.

Food Habits

  • Reduce excessive non-vegetarian food consumption.
  • Include more plant-based and locally sourced food.
  • Avoid food wastage.

Cooking & Fuel

  • Use energy-efficient cooking methods like induction.
  • Optimize LPG usage and avoid unnecessary gas consumption.

Flight Travel

  • Avoid frequent flights when alternatives like trains are available.
  • Prefer virtual meetings for short business trips.

Online Shopping & Lifestyle

  • Reduce unnecessary online shopping.
  • Combine orders to minimize delivery emissions.
  • Choose sustainable and eco-friendly products.
